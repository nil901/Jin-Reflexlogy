## 🚀 Quick Tips

- **Spacing**: Use 16px, 12px, 8px rhythm
- **Colors**: Purple gradient for sections
- **Cards**: White with 16px radius
- **Text**: 11px for labels, 16px for headers
- **Grid**: Always 3 columns
- **Images**: Contain fit with padding
- **Shadows**: Subtle, not harsh
- **Navigation**: InkWell with ripple
